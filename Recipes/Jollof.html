JOLLOF RICE

Home

Jollof Rice is a popular West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, onions, and spices. It's known for its rich flavor, vibrant color, and is often served with fried plantains, chicken, or beef.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups of white rice
  • 4 cups of water
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil or butter (optional)

Steps:

  1. Rinse the rice in cold water until the water runs clear.
  2. In a pot, add rice, water, salt, and oil/butter.
  3. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat.
  4. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and cover with a lid.
  5. Simmer for about 15–20 minutes until the water is absorbed and rice is tender.
  6. Turn off the heat and let the rice sit covered for 5 minutes.
  7. Fluff with a fork and serve.
