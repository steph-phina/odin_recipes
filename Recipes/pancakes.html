Pancake recipe

Home

Pancakes are fluffy, golden-brown breakfast favorites made from a simple batter of flour, eggs, and milk. They’re quick to prepare and delicious with syrup, fruits, or butter.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup of all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon of sugar
  • 1 teaspoon of baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon of baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon of salt
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup of milk
  • 2 tablespoons of melted butter or oil

Steps:

  1. In a bowl, mix flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  2. In another bowl, whisk together egg, milk, and melted butter.
  3. Combine the wet and dry ingredients until just mixed.
  4. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour batter onto the pan to form small circles.
  6. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, then flip and cook the other side.
  7. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.
